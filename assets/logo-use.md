# Ox Logo Usage Guide

## Clear Space

Maintain a clear space around the bull mark equal to the height of one horn (approximately 1/4 of the mark's total height). No text, icon, or graphic element may enter this zone.

## Rotation

The logo must never be rotated. It is always upright.

## Outlines

Never apply an outline to the logo. The mark is designed as clean strokes and solid fills only.

## Gradient Fills

Do not use gradient fills on the bull mark. The only accent color allowed is ember-orange (#FF7A1A) for the eyes. All strokes remain #EDEAE4.

## Minimum Sizes

| Use Case | Minimum Size |
|---|---|
| Favicon | 16x16 px |
| Toolbar icon | 24x24 px |
| App icon | 512x512 px |
| Splash screen | 4096x4096 px (4K) |

## Color Variants

- **Full color**: #EDEAE4 strokes, #FF7A1A eyes
- **Monochrome white**: all #FFFFFF strokes and eyes
- **Monochrome black**: all #000000 strokes and eyes

## Lockups

### Horizontal lockup

Bull mark on the left, wordmark "OX" on the right, vertically centered. Minimum spacing between mark and wordmark equals one horn height.

### Stacked lockup

Bull mark on top, wordmark "OX" below, horizontally centered. Minimum vertical spacing equals one horn height.

## Do Not Use

- Do not stretch or distort proportions
- Do not recolor strokes to any color other than #EDEAE4, #FFFFFF, or #000000
- Do not apply drop shadows or glow effects
- Do not place on low-contrast backgrounds (minimum contrast ratio 4.5:1)
- Do not animate the horns or eyes
- Do not use the mark without the wordmark in editorial contexts unless using the isolated mark-only variant
