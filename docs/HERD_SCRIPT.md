# The Meadow: how the ox's world lives and runs the island

October 18, 2026. The owner's brief: the characters perfect in how they move;
a little forest of animals that run OX Island in their own way; when nothing
needs you, they play. This is the script the island follows
(`desktop/island/Sources/OxIsland/FamilyPasture.swift`).

## The place

A strip of meadow inside the island's left card, drawn back to front:

1. **Sky** that follows your clock: dawn pink (5–7), day blue (7–18), dusk
   orange (18–20), night navy with stars and a moon (20–5).
2. **Far trees**: a row of pines and round trees along the back.
3. **The meadow**: grass, flowers, the **pond** (the trough) on the right.
4. **Near bushes** at the front corners; an animal walking behind one is
   partly hidden, so the forest has depth.
5. **Weather** from the island's day strip: rain falls when it rains, snow
   when it snows; birds fly over on clear days.

## The cast, and the job each one does

| Who | Looks | Job on the island |
|---|---|---|
| **Elder** | the first ox: the only dark one, bigger horns, a grey scarf, drawn like the rest of the cast | Keeps time and limits. Naps most. Worries when a plan passes 80 % of its window. At night he leads everyone to sleep. |
| **Ox** | cream and charcoal, the main character | Your own ox runs. While one works the ox leaves to work (it is in the card beside its run); when it ends it comes back and hops. When something needs you, it walks to the front and looks at you. |
| **Puff, Pebble, Dot** | the white friends | Each stands in for another agent session (Claude Code, Codex…). While that session works, that friend is away "at work"; it comes back when the session goes quiet. |
| **Maple** | a chestnut horse with a dark mane (Quaternius' CC0 horse, its own coat) | The messenger. When mail arrives, a teammate machine raises an alert, or a hire is waiting, Maple gallops in from the left and stops at the front with a note; tap Maple to open it. |
| **Fern** | a deer (Quaternius CC0, its own coat) | The shy one. Drinks most and wanders to the edge of the trees; she is the first to look up when something needs you. |
| **Rusty** | a small red fox (Quaternius CC0) | The playful one. Plays most, naps in between, and chases whoever starts a game. |
| **Birds** | two small birds | The weather. They fly across on clear days and stay away in the rain. |

## What they do when nothing needs you

Each animal picks its own next thing, so the meadow never loops the same way:

- wander to a spot, graze, drink at the pond (two at a time), nap;
- play: two meet, head-butt, then one runs and the other chases;
- zoomies: a short gallop, then a stop;
- explore: walk behind the far trees and come back out;
- the elder naps twice as often; Pebble plays most; Dot drinks most.

Tap an animal: it blinks, hops and says something ("moo!", "slurp!", or
"zzz… what?" if you woke it).

## What they do when something happens

| Something happens | The meadow |
|---|---|
| Music plays (Spotify, Music) | everyone bobs in time, now and then a hop |
| An approval or a question waits | the whole herd stops and looks toward you |
| A run fails | the ox gets dizzy for a moment |
| A run finishes | everyone hops |
| A plan passes 80 % | the elder worries |
| Mail, a machine alert, a hire waiting | Maple gallops in with the note |
| Night (20:00–05:00) | they doze, the barn's lantern glows, stars come out |
| Rain | they gather under the trees |

## Rules

- Never louder than the work: in the closed notch nothing moves but the ox.
- Never black friends; the elder is the only dark one (he was the first ox).
- A friend away at work is not drawn; the card says who is away.
- Every reaction lasts a few seconds, then the animal goes back to its own day.
